Very fun and very sad announcements from your friends in LaterGator:

**Fun:** Our first full-length album, "It's All Temporary", will be coming out on June 5th! We're seriously so proud of this record and we're sure you'll love it too. This was the first time we've gotten to work in a real studio and in person with the GOAT Nick Starrantino, and we made the absolute best sendoff record we could dream up.

**Sad:** This album will be our only album, and our final show will be here in Indy on September 5th. More info to come on the show!

We've had an incredible 3 years as a band and we wouldn't trade that time for the world. It's not easy finding such an incredible community of band members, friends, and goofy midwest emo music lovers, but we've been lucky enough to make so many great memories with you all in our time as a band. 

We would love to keep making records and playing shows here, but unfortunately we're hopelessly addicted to paying bills and buying groceries and sometimes that means moving across the country for work. 

We love the thought of regrouping someday after moving, but we can't count on having any more shows beyond these next few months. We're going to make the absolute most of it and hope you can join us for a show or two along the way!
